# 16. Interviews, assessments and scorecards: user guide

Phase 7 covers what happens after shortlisting: **written tests, practical assessments and interviews**, each with a **panel** that scores the applicant on a **scorecard**. GREL requires the department's **Head of Department** on every panel, and the HOD reviews the scorecards.

## 1. Schedule a session (Recruitment Officer or HR Manager)

Open a **shortlisted** application (from *Applications* or the shortlisting board) and click **Schedule an interview or assessment**.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Interview, written test or practical assessment |
| **How** | In person, video call or phone call |
| **Date and time** | GMT (Ghana time). A session that already took place (up to yesterday) can be recorded too. |
| **Duration** and **where** | Room and building, or the video link |
| **Instructions** | Shown to the applicant, e.g. *bring your original certificates* |
| **Panel** | 2 to 8 GREL staff. The department's **HOD is ticked for you and must stay**. Choose one **chair**. The applicant can never sit on their own panel. |
| **Scorecard criteria** | One per line, `name \| weight`; the weights must add up to **100**. Five sensible defaults are filled in. |

Click **Schedule and send invitations**. Then:
* the applicant is emailed the date, place and instructions, and sees them on their tracker;
* each panel member is emailed;
* the application moves to **Interview**.

An applicant can have several **rounds**, for example a written test followed by an interview.

## 1a. Interview guide (13 November 2026, docs/36)

On the vacancy page, **Interview guide → Open**. The Recruitment Officer or HR Manager lists the questions the panel should ask, by category (technical, behavioural, situational, competency, practical assessment), each with *what a strong answer shows*, then clicks **Save the guide**. With AI switched on (*AI settings & usage*), **Draft questions** fills the form with suggestions from the vacancy's description and requirements, to edit before saving. Panel members see the guide, folded, at the top of the interview page. Applicants never see it.

## 2. Score (panel members)

Any GREL staff member can sit on a panel. Open **Menu → Interviews**:
* **My panels** lists your sessions.
* **Scorecards to do** lists sessions that have started and still need your scorecard. The red number in the menu and the *My tasks* list on Home show the same.

On the session page, the scorecard **opens when the session starts**:
1. Rate each criterion **1 to 5** (1 poor, 2 below expectations, 3 meets, 4 above, 5 outstanding).
2. Note **strengths** and **concerns**. Concerns are required if you do not recommend the applicant.
3. Choose an overall **recommendation**: strongly recommend, recommend, recommend with reservations, or do not recommend.
4. Click **Submit scorecard**.

GRSMS works out the **weighted score out of 100**: each rating ÷ 5 × the criterion's weight, added up. For example, 4/5 on a 60-weight criterion plus 5/5 on a 40-weight one gives 48 + 40 = **88**.

**Independent scoring:** panel members see the other scorecards **only after submitting their own**, so nobody is swayed. You can update your scorecard until the Recruitment Officer validates the scores.

## 2a. Panel replies and quorum (12 November 2026, docs/35)

* Every panel member is asked to **accept or decline** the invitation: on the session page and in *My tasks* on Home (*Panel invitation: … Accept or decline*). A decline needs a reason.
* **Quorum:** at least **3** panel members, including the department's **HOD** (or a delegate from the same department) and an **HR Manager**. Scheduling checks this, and scores **cannot be validated** until quorum is met on the day (members marked absent don't count).
* **When someone declines,** the scheduler, the vacancy's recruiter and the HR Managers are emailed (*Quorum at risk* when it is). The RO or HR Manager clicks **Replace** next to that member and picks someone else; the new member is invited and checked for clashes. A replacement for the HOD must be from the same department and is shown as **HOD delegate**. If the chair is replaced, the new member becomes chair.
* The session page shows each member's reply and either what quorum still needs or *Quorum met*.
* The rule can be switched off in **System settings → Workflow → Enforce interview quorum** (on by default). Sessions scheduled before 12 November are not held to it.

## 3. Score validation (Recruitment Officer)

*Added 9 November 2026.* Every set of scores is checked by a Recruitment Officer **before** anyone else relies on it.

1. When **every panel member who took part** has submitted, the session shows **Awaiting score validation** and the vacancy's Recruitment Officer is emailed. It also appears in their *Your to-dos* on Home, in the red number next to *Interviews*, and on the **To validate** tab.
2. The Recruitment Officer opens the session and checks each scorecard: every criterion rated, comments that support the ratings, concerns given where the applicant is not recommended.
3. Then either:
   * **Validate the scores** (optional note). The session becomes **Completed (scores validated)**, the scores are final, and the panel and the HOD are emailed. **GRSMS then takes you straight to the candidate's hiring case** ([17 Hiring](17-hiring.md)): the next step is the hiring recommendation; or
   * **Return a scorecard** to its panel member with what needs correcting. They are emailed the reason, see *Your next step: correct your scorecard*, and submit it again; the session then comes back for validation.
4. **A panel member who did not take part?** Mark them **Absent** or **Excused** with a reason, so their scorecard is not waited for (Undo is possible). **The Head of Department cannot be marked absent**: their scorecard is required; reschedule if they could not attend.

**Rules:** the Recruitment Officer **never changes scores**. **Any Recruitment Officer** can validate, also one who sat on the panel, but nobody can return their own scorecard. If the vacancy's owner is not a Recruitment Officer, every Recruitment Officer is emailed and gets the to-do. HR Managers do not validate.

**Until the scores are validated** they are seen only by panel members who have submitted their own and by Recruitment Officers. The HOD (unless on the panel and scored), the MD, HR Managers and HR Administrators, the **interview results** and the **hiring case** show validated scores only, and a **hiring recommendation needs at least one validated interview**.

## 4. Other actions (Recruitment Officer or HR Manager)

| Action | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Reschedule** | Before any scorecard is in | New time and place; the applicant and panel are emailed |
| **Applicant did not attend** | After it started | Recorded on the application's history |
| **Cancel** | Any time before the scores are validated, with a reason | The applicant and panel are emailed |

## 5. Review the results (HOD, recruiters, HR Manager, MD)

* **Session page:** every scorecard side by side, with ratings per criterion, weighted scores, recommendations and comments, plus the panel average and who validated the scores.
* **Interview results** (from the session page, the application page or the shortlisting board): every applicant for the vacancy who had a session, ranked by their **average validated interview score**, with their screening score, each round and how the panel recommended. A session still awaiting validation shows no score. This is the starting point for the hiring recommendation (Phase 8).

## What the applicant sees

The date, time, place, how long it takes and your instructions, on their tracker and in their email. **Never** scores, ratings, recommendations or comments.
