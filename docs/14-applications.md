# 14. Candidate portal and applications: user guide

Phase 5 lets people **apply online** for vacancies advertised on the careers page, and **follow their application** step by step. GREL staff use the same pages to apply for internal vacancies. The recruitment team receives the applications in GRSMS, with the applicant's details and documents.

## For applicants (candidates and GREL staff)

### 1. Create an account (candidates)
On the careers page (`/Careers`) or the sign-in page, click **Create a candidate account**. Confirm your email with the link we send, then sign in. GREL staff simply sign in with their usual account.

### 2. Fill in "My profile" once
**Menu → My profile.** Before you can apply you need:
* a **phone number**,
* your **highest education**,
* your **CV** (PDF or Word, up to 10 MB). Uploading a new CV replaces the old one.

You can also add your field of study, years of experience, current job, skills and certifications (one per line), certificates (up to 10 files), a LinkedIn link and a short "About you". The more you fill in, the better the recruitment team can match you to the job.

### 3. Apply
1. Open a job on the careers page and click **Apply now**. (Not signed in? Click **Sign in to apply**. You come back to the same job afterwards.)
2. Check **What we will send**: your profile and documents. Click **Edit my profile** to change anything.
3. Say **where you saw the job**, add an optional **covering message**, and tick the confirmation.
4. Click **Send application**. You get an email with your reference number, e.g. **APP-2026-00001**.

Rules:
* You can apply **once per vacancy**.
* Jobs marked **GREL staff only** accept applications from staff only.
* Applications close when the advert or the vacancy closes.
* A copy of your profile and documents is kept **with the application**. Changing your profile later does not change an application you have already sent.

### 4. Follow your application
**Menu → My applications** lists everything you have applied for. Open one to see the **tracker**:

```
Received → Under review → Shortlisted → Interview → Offer → Hired
```

The tracker and *Updates* list show where your application is. If it is not taken further, the next step shows a ✗ and you receive a polite email. The recruitment team's internal reasons are never shown to applicants.

You can **withdraw** an application at any time before an offer. A withdrawn application cannot be sent again for the same vacancy.

## For the recruitment team

**Menu → Applications** (Recruitment Officers, HR Managers, the MD and HR Administrators see all; Heads of Department see their departments').

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **New** | Received, not yet reviewed, oldest first |
| **Under review** | Being reviewed, oldest first |
| **All** | Everything, newest first, with a status filter |

**Filters:** search (name, email, application or vacancy number), division, department, period (date applied), applicants (GREL staff / external), source (where they saw the job) and status. Each vacancy page shows its number of applications with a **View applications** link, and the Home dashboard has an **Applications** card.

**Open an application** to see the applicant's details as sent (contact, education, experience, skills and certifications), their covering message, and their **CV and certificates**. Files always download; they never open inside the site.

**Actions** (Recruitment Officer or HR Manager):
* **Start review**: the applicant's tracker moves to *Under review*.
* **Mark not successful**: requires an **internal reason**, which the applicant never sees. The applicant gets a polite email.

Nobody can act on their **own** application: another reviewer must handle it. Heads of Department can read their departments' applications; **shortlisting, with the HOD taking part, arrives in Phase 6.**

**Emails:** the vacancy's recruiter is emailed for every new application and every withdrawal.

## Privacy

* Applicants only ever see their own profile and applications.
* Staff see applications only through the Applications screens, limited by role and department.
* Documents are stored outside the website, under random names, and are only reachable through permission-checked download links.
* Every change is in the audit trail.
