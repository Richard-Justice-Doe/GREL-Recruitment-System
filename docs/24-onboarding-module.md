# 24. Employee onboarding: user guide

For **HR (Recruitment Officers and HR Managers), Heads of Department, task owners and new employees**. No technical knowledge needed.

Onboarding starts the moment a candidate **accepts the offer** and ends when the new employee is an **active GREL employee** with a staff account. (After that, probation begins; probation management is added in Phase 21.)

> This guide describes the onboarding workflow added in Phase 18 (6 November 2026). The appointment letter, first-day details and the departmental checklist work as described in [18 Appointment letters and onboarding](18-onboarding.md); this page covers the stages and the new-hire documents around them.

**Menu → Onboarding** (staff). New employees use **My applications → their application → Upload my new-hire documents**.

---

## The stages

```
 HR starts             HR Manager            New employee            HR                    HR Manager        Departments           HR
 ─────────             ──────────            ────────────            ──                    ──────────        ───────────           ──
 1. Draft ──letter──► 2. Awaiting ──submit──► 3. Awaiting ──verified──► 4. Awaiting ──approve──► 5. Awaiting ──done──► 6. Awaiting ──activate──► ✅ Completed
                         employee               document                 HR approval            departmental          activation
                         submission             verification                 │                  induction
                            ▲                       │                        │
                            └── rejected documents ─┘ ◄── returned for verification
 (any stage before Completed) ── cancel ──► ❌ Cancelled
```

| Stage | What happens | Who acts |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | HR has started the onboarding (first-day details, checklist). | HR Manager issues the appointment letter |
| **Awaiting employee submission** | The new employee signs the letter and uploads the new-hire documents. | New employee clicks **Submit to HR** (HR can submit for them) |
| **Awaiting document verification** | HR checks each document against the original. | Recruitment Officer or HR Manager |
| **Awaiting HR approval** | The HR Manager approves the new hire's records. | HR Manager (not the person who verified the documents) |
| **Awaiting departmental induction** | The departments complete their checklist tasks (IT accounts, workspace, induction, ...). | Task owners, the HOD, HR |
| **Awaiting activation** | HR records the staff number from payroll. | Recruitment Officer or HR Manager |
| **Completed** | The account is a GREL **staff account** (Employee role, department and site set). | — |

The onboarding page shows the stages as a progress bar, the **expected completion date** (30 days after the start date by default) and a **History** of every step with who did it and when. **Full change log** opens the audit trail.

## For the new employee

1. You receive an email when your **appointment letter** is ready. Sign in, open **My applications**, then your application.
2. **Read and sign** the letter (type your full name).
3. Click **My personal record**, fill in the form (below), click **Save my personal record**, then **sign the declaration** at the top of the page.
4. Click **Upload my new-hire documents**. For each document, choose the file and click **Upload**. You can add several files to one document (for example, several school certificates). Accepted: **PDF, JPG or PNG**, up to 10 MB each.
5. Documents marked *(if applicable)* can be marked **not applicable** instead, with a short reason (for example "Not married", "Ghanaian citizen").
6. When the letter and the personal record are signed and everything is uploaded, click **Submit to HR**.

If HR rejects a document, you get an email listing what needs attention, and the document shows **Rejected** with HR's reason. Upload it again and click **Submit to HR** again. Verified documents are locked. HR can also send your onboarding back to correct your personal record; the email says what to change.

### Your personal record (Employee Personal Record Declaration)

Your name, staff number, department, position and service date are filled in from your onboarding. You give:

| Section | What to fill in |
|---|---|
| **Employee information** | Qualification, SSNIT number, date of birth (your age is worked out from it), residential address, home town, region, tribe, religion, email, mobile number, any disability (and support you need at work), visible marks |
| **Languages spoken** | Each language and how well you speak it: *very fluent*, *fluent* or *passable* |
| **Bank accounts** | Bank, branch and account number. Mark one as **primary**: your salary is paid into it |
| **Family records** | Spouse (if any): name, occupation, date of birth, address, telephone. Children: name, gender, date of birth, place of birth, where they live now. Dependants: name, relationship, age. Add as many rows as you need |
| **Next of kin** | Name, relationship, telephone, date of birth, address |
| **Emergency contact** | Name, relationship and one or two telephone numbers (up to three contacts) |
| **Beneficiaries** | Name, relationship, date of birth and **share in %**. The shares must add up to **exactly 100%**: the page shows the running total, and the record is not saved otherwise |

**The declaration.** Tick *"I declare that the above information is accurate and correct to the best of my knowledge"* and type your full name, then **Sign**. GRSMS records your name, the date and time and your IP address, with a frozen copy of everything you declared and a fingerprint of it. **If you change anything afterwards, you must sign again**; every earlier signature is kept, unchanged.

You can change your personal record only while it is your turn (before you submit, or when HR sends the onboarding back to you). After that you can still read it.

### The documents (New Hire Administrative Records)

| Required | If applicable |
|---|---|
| Application letter · CV · Passport picture · Recommendation letters · Medical report · Police clearance · School certificates · Birth certificate · Ghana Card · SSNIT card · Bank confirmation letter | Marriage certificate · Children's birth certificates · Dependants' photographs · Passport biodata page · Non-citizen card |

## For HR

### Starting and the letter
As before: **Start onboarding** from the hiring case or **Onboarding → Waiting to start**; the HR Manager **issues the appointment letter**. Issuing the letter for the first time moves the onboarding to **Awaiting employee submission** and emails the new employee (template *Onboarding: letter and documents*).

### Verifying the documents
When the new employee submits, the vacancy's recruiter and whoever started the onboarding are emailed. On the onboarding page, under **New-hire documents**:
* open each file (it always downloads; nothing opens inside the site);
* click **Verified against the original**, or type a reason and click **Reject**. For a "not applicable" declaration, click **Accept** or reject it with a reason;
* **Open the personal record** to read the new employee's Personal Record Declaration (read-only, printable) and the signature history;
* if anything was rejected, or the personal record needs correcting, click **Send back to the new employee**. A message is required when no document was rejected. The new employee is emailed your reasons and message;
* when every document is verified or accepted, click **All documents verified: send to the HR Manager**. The HR Managers are emailed.

HR can also upload a document for the new employee (for example a scan of an original brought in on paper) until the HR Manager approves, and can **Submit for the new employee**.

### HR approval
An HR Manager ticks the **departmental induction areas** that apply to this new employee, then **approves** or **returns for verification** with comments. **The HR Manager who marked the documents verified cannot approve the same onboarding**; another HR Manager must.

On approval, each ticked area's tasks are added to the checklist, given to the area's owner, and due a week after the induction starts (the start date, or today if later). The task owners and the HOD are emailed that the induction can begin. A task already on the checklist is not added twice.

### Departmental induction
The checklist on the onboarding page is grouped by area:

| Area | Example tasks (built in; changeable on **Induction areas**) |
|---|---|
| Human Resource | Company orientation; policies review (plus the HR preparation tasks from the start: personal file, induction programme, payroll, benefits) |
| Corporate Information Technology | Email setup; AD account creation; system access (plus the IT tasks from the start) |
| Corporate Procurement · Corporate Supply Chain · Corporate Finance | A briefing on the department's procedures |
| Factory Operations · TBU Operations · Stores Operations · Engineering | A site or workshop induction (stores: stock-issue briefing; engineering: permit-to-work) |
| Security | Access card; security induction |
| Health & Safety | Safety training; PPE issue and emergency procedures |
| Administration | Office, transport and canteen arrangements |
| Own department (HOD) | Workspace, tools and PPE; department induction; probation objectives |

Each task shows its **owner**, **assigned date**, **due date** and, once done, who did it and when. Owners (or the HOD for department tasks, or HR) mark it **Done** or **Not needed** (with a reason).

**HR verifies the induction:** a task marked done by anyone outside HR shows *waiting for HR to verify*. HR clicks **Verify** after checking it really was done. A task HR marks done counts as verified.

HR can also **add a task** to any area (with an optional description), reassign one, change its due date, or remove one still to do.

### Induction areas (HR Administrator)
**Menu → Organisation → Induction areas** lists the twelve areas. For each area:
* **Owner**: who receives its tasks. With no owner, they go to HR (the vacancy's recruiter); Corporate IT with no owner goes to the Head of the IT department;
* **Ticked by default**: whether the HR Manager sees it already ticked when approving. Out of the box: Human Resource, Corporate IT, Security, and Health & Safety;
* **Tasks**: one per line.

Changes apply to onboardings approved from then on, never to tasks already created.

### Induction and activation
* When every checklist item is **Done** or **Not needed**, **every task done outside HR has been verified**, and the start date has come, click **Induction complete: ready to activate**.
* Enter the **staff number** from payroll and click **Activate the employee**. The new employee's account becomes a staff account: the Employee role is added, the Candidate role removed, and the staff number, department and site are set. The new employee (template *Onboarding: staff account active*) and the HOD are emailed.
* At the same moment the employee's **electronic personnel file** is opened and filled (below).

### The electronic personnel file
**Menu → Personnel files** (Recruitment Officers and HR Managers; the permission *Open personnel files*), or **Personnel file** on the onboarding page.

Each employee has one file, numbered e.g. **PF-2026-0001**, with seven folders. These are records in the database, not Windows folders:

| Folder | Filed automatically at activation |
|---|---|
| Recruitment Documents | The application's files (CV, certificates, cover letter as sent); application letter, CV and recommendation letters |
| Employee Records | The signed Personal Record Declaration; school certificates, marriage certificate, children's birth certificates, dependants' photographs |
| Identification Records | Passport picture, birth certificate, Ghana Card, SSNIT card, passport biodata page, non-citizen card |
| Compliance Records | Medical report, police clearance, bank confirmation letter |
| Onboarding Records | The onboarding and induction record: every task, who did it and who verified it |
| Probation Records | Added by probation management (a later phase) |
| Generated Letters | The appointment letter, frozen exactly as it was signed |

Only **verified** new-hire documents are filed. Files are not copied: the personnel file points to the same securely stored file. Click a file to download it, or a letter to read and print it (**Print or save as PDF**). HR can **add a document** to any folder.

An onboarding completed before personnel files existed shows **Open the personnel file** for HR, which opens and fills it the same way.

### Notes and cancelling
**HR notes** are for HR only (never shown to the new employee). **Cancel onboarding** needs a reason and works at any stage before Completed.

## Who can see what

| Person | Sees |
|---|---|
| HR (Recruitment Officer, HR Manager) | Every onboarding, the new-hire documents and files, the personal record, HR notes, and the personnel files |
| Head of Department | Their department's onboardings and checklist, **not** the new-hire documents or the personal record (Ghana Card, medical report, family, bank and beneficiary details) |
| A task owner (e.g. an induction area owner) | The onboarding, while they have a task on it |
| The new employee | Their own letter, first-day information, documents and personal record, in their portal |

Every upload, verification, rejection and stage change is in the audit trail. Files are stored on the server under random names outside the website's public folder, with a SHA-256 fingerprint.
