# 39. Guided workflow: where everything stands, at a glance

Added 15 November 2026, on top of docs/37 CR5 (process guide, Action Center).

The aim: a new user with no training can see, on every record:
* where the process is;
* what is done and what is pending;
* who must act and who approves next;
* how long it has waited;
* what happens next.

---

## 1. What is on every record page

**Pages:** request, vacancy, advert, application, interview, hiring case, onboarding.

**The process guide** (top of the page):

| | |
|---|---|
| **Workflow completion** | A bar and a percentage (steps done out of all steps), with the health badge: 🟢 *On track*, 🟡 *Approaching deadline*, 🔴 *Overdue*. Deadlines come from *System settings → Workflow targets*. |
| **Timeline** | Done steps have a **green tick**, the current step is **blue**, steps to come are **grey**. A stopped process shows a red cross. On a phone the timeline scrolls sideways, starting at the step before the current one. |
| **Next action** | E.g. *Waiting for the HR Manager's approval*. |
| **Current owner** | The role and the people, e.g. *HR Manager: Patrick Badoo*. |
| **Next approver** | When an approval is pending. |
| **Pending for** | E.g. *4 days 6 hours*, with the health badge. |
| **Expected by** | The date the target falls on (red when passed). |
| **Follow up with** | *To move this along, follow up with: HR Manager (names) · pending approval for 4 days 6 hours.* Below it: *Once done, the next step is MD approval.* |
| **Last action** | What, who and when. |

**Breadcrumbs** start at Home on every staff page, e.g. *Home › Candidates › Applications › APP-2026-0012*, and *Home › Administration › Divisions* on administration screens.

## 2. The two journeys

**Requisition** (request and vacancy pages), since 17 Nov 2026: request submitted → Department Head approval → Recruitment Officer review → Managing Director approval (the last approval) → vacancy creation → advertisement → candidate applications → selection and hiring → positions filled. After the MD, the owner is the Recruitment Officer and no step names an approver.

**Candidate** (application, interview, hiring case pages): applied → screening → interview → hire approval → offer → onboarding → employee. The panel members accept or decline their invitation. A decline notifies the Recruitment Officer at once, and they choose a replacement (Wave 1, docs/37 C1).

Two decisions stay as they are:
* **Hire approval is the Recruitment Officer's alone** (decision of 9 November 2026). The brief's "hiring authority" step is that approval.
* **Onboarding has no separate HR approval** (decision of 10 November 2026). The Recruitment Officer verifies the documents, then the staff account is activated.

## 3. The onboarding journey

On the new employee's pages it appears as **"Welcome to your onboarding journey"**: *My application*, *My new-hire documents*, *My personal record* and the appointment letter. The staff onboarding page shows the same stages in staff wording; it replaces the earlier 1–6 stepper.

| Stage | Who | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Offer accepted | New employee | ✅ |
| 2 Appointment letter | Recruitment Officer prepares it, the new employee signs | *Being prepared* / *Ready: read and sign it* / *Signed* |
| 3 Personal information | New employee | Bio data, contacts, address, next of kin, emergency contacts, bank account |
| 4 Document upload | New employee | *4 of 6 uploaded*, rejected ones to upload again, the medical report's status |
| 5 Recruitment Officer verification | Recruitment Officer | *Waiting for verification*, *x of y verified* |
| 6 Staff account activation | Recruitment Officer / HR Manager | *Waiting for activation* → *Staff account active* |
| 7 IT set-up | IT | The live tasks: email account and system access, computer / phone |
| 8 Department induction | Supervisor / Head of Department | The live induction tasks |
| 9 Onboarding complete | HR | *Welcome to GREL!* |

* Stages 2–4 can be in progress at the same time.
* The card under the timeline names the new employee's own next step first.
* *See all 9 stages* opens each stage's status, owner, details and tasks.

## 4. My Action Center

**Menu → Action Center.** Also linked from Home.

* **Counts at the top:**
  * 🔴 **Overdue:** past the workflow target or the expected date;
  * 🟡 **Due today**;
  * **Coming up**;
  * 🟢 **Completed**: what you did in the last 14 days.
* **The actions in those sections,** each with its priority (High / Medium / Low), how long it has waited and a button to the page. Actions include approving requisitions, reviewing candidates, interviews and scorecards, verifying documents, offers and onboarding tasks.
* **The priority filter** still works.

## 5. Notifications

* **Every workflow email has a bell notification.**
* **Handovers carry a "next step" block:** *✅ Department Head approval completed. Next step: Recruitment Officer review. Responsible: Recruitment Officer.* This applies to:
  * request approvals (to the requester);
  * the vacancy published;
  * the advert live;
  * the offer accepted (*Next step: employee onboarding*);
  * the appointment letter signed;
  * the staff account activated (*Next step: department induction and IT set-up*).
* **The person who must act** is emailed, as before, e.g. *Vacancy request awaiting your approval*, *Candidate documents need verification*, *Interview scorecard pending submission*.
