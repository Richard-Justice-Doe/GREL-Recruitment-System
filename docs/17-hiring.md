# 17. Background checks, hiring recommendation and offers: user guide

Phase 8 takes an interviewed applicant to a signed-off hire:

1. **background checks**;
2. the **hiring recommendation**, with the Head of Department's endorsement and HR and MD approval;
3. the **offer**, which the candidate accepts or declines online.

Everything for one applicant is on their **hiring case** page. Open it from the application page (*Hiring: checks, recommendation and offer*), from *Interview results*, or from **Menu → Hiring and offers**.

## 1. Background checks (Recruitment Officer or HR Manager)

From the interview stage on, add each check with **Add check**:

| Check | Example |
|---|---|
| References | *Mr K. Owusu, Plant Manager, Tema Rubber Ltd* |
| Education / certificates verification | *BSc Chemical Engineering, KNUST, 2019* |
| Previous employment | *Tema Rubber Ltd, 2019-2025* |
| Criminal record (police report) | |
| Medical fitness | |

A check starts as **Pending**. Record the outcome: **Cleared**, **Cleared with concerns**, or **Failed**. Concerns and failures need a note.

**Before an offer:** at least one check, none pending, none failed.

## 2. The hiring recommendation (the hiring report)

The recruiter fills in the **proposed monthly salary**, **start date** and **why this candidate** (interview results, references, fit), then clicks **Recommend**. This needs at least one **completed** interview or assessment.

The recommendation then goes through three decisions, each recorded with who decided, when and their comment:

| Step | Who | Choices |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The department's **Head of Department** (GREL requirement: HODs take part in the final hiring recommendation) | **Endorse**, or **Do not endorse** with a reason |
| 2 | An **HR Manager** | **Approve** or **Reject** (with a reason). If the HOD did **not** endorse, approving needs a written reason (an *override*). |
| 3 | The **Managing Director** | **Approve** (final) or **Reject** (with a reason) |

Each person is emailed when it is their turn and sees the task in *My tasks* on the Home page. The **Awaiting my decision** tab on *Hiring and offers* lists them too.

**Safeguards:**
* The person who proposed the hire cannot approve it.
* The HR approver cannot also give the MD approval.
* Nobody acts on their own application.

The recruiter can **withdraw** an open recommendation, with a reason. After a rejection or withdrawal, a new recommendation can be made.

The recommendation is the **hiring report**. It records the division, department and requesting HOD, so hiring can be reported by division and department.

## 3. The offer (Recruitment Officer or HR Manager)

Once the MD has approved, the checks are complete and the vacancy still has a free position, the hiring case shows **Send offer**:
* **Salary**: up to the approved amount.
* **Start date**.
* **Reply by**: within 30 days, and no later than the start date. The default is 7 days.
* **Other terms** for the letter, such as probation or benefits.

The candidate is emailed, and the application moves to **Offer**.

**Positions:** a vacancy with *N* positions can have at most *N* offers that are sent or accepted at the same time.

The recruiter can **withdraw** a sent offer, with a reason; the candidate is told. Offers not answered by their reply-by date **expire automatically** (hourly check). In both cases the applicant goes back to the interview stage, so another offer or another candidate can follow.

## 4. The candidate's reply

On *My applications*, the candidate sees **Your offer → Read and reply to my offer**. The **offer letter** shows:
* position, department, place of work, reporting manager;
* monthly salary, start date, terms, and the reply-by date.

The letter can be printed. Then the candidate either:
* ticks *I accept on these terms* and clicks **Accept the offer**. The application becomes **Hired**, and the recruiter and HOD are emailed. The candidate gets a welcome email.
* or clicks **Decline the offer** (reason optional). The application becomes **Offer declined**, and the recruiter is emailed.

Next, HR starts the onboarding and issues the appointment letter: see [18 Onboarding](18-onboarding.md).
