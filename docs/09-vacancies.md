# 9. Vacancies: user guide

For **Recruitment Officers and HR Managers** (Heads of Department can view their own department's vacancies). No technical knowledge needed.

A vacancy is the position as it will be advertised. Every vacancy comes from a **fully approved recruitment request** (see the [recruitment requests guide](08-recruitment-requests.md)), so nothing can be advertised without the HOD → Recruitment → HR → MD approvals.

```
 Recruitment Officer                      HR Manager                   (automatic or by hand)
 ───────────────────                      ──────────                   ──────────────────────
 1. Draft ── submit ──► 2. Pending approval ── approve ──► 3. Published ── closing date passes ──► 4. Closed ── archive ──► 5. Archived
      ▲                         │                                ▲                                   │
      └──── return (comments) ──┘                                └──────── reopen with a new date ───┘
```

**Menu → Recruitment → Vacancies**

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Published vacancies, closing soonest first. A red label marks the last 3 days. |
| **Pending approval** | Vacancies waiting for the HR Manager, oldest first |
| **Approved requests awaiting a vacancy** | Approved recruitment requests that still need a vacancy. Recruitment Officers start here. |
| **All** | Every vacancy, with filters for status, department and search |

---

## Recruitment Officers

### Create a vacancy
1. Open the **Approved requests awaiting a vacancy** tab (or the approved request itself) and click **Create vacancy**.
2. The form is filled in from the request. The department, number of positions and employment type are **fixed**, because that is what was approved. Check and complete:
   * **Job title**, **who may apply** (internal staff, the public, or both), **unit**, **site**, **reporting manager** (the HOD by default);
   * **Job description**: what the applicant will read, covering the job purpose and the main duties;
   * **Requirements**: minimum education and field of study, minimum years and type of experience, and the **skills** and **certifications**. Put **one per line**, separating *essential* (must have) from *desirable* (nice to have). These lists are used later to rank applicants automatically, so keep each item short and specific ("NEBOSH IGC", not "a safety certificate of some kind");
   * **Salary range** (monthly, GHS) and whether to **show it on adverts**;
   * **Closing date**: the last day applicants can apply. It is two weeks ahead by default.
3. Click **Save draft**. The vacancy gets its number, e.g. **VAC-2026-0001**.

### Submit for approval
Open the draft and click **Submit for approval**. The HR Managers are emailed. If the vacancy comes back **returned for changes**, the comments appear in a yellow box at the top. Click **Edit**, fix the issues and submit again.

### After it is published
* **Close now** stops new applications early (e.g. enough good applicants).
* When the closing date passes, the vacancy **closes by itself** within the hour. Applications are accepted for the whole of the closing day. You'll get an email when it closes.
* **Reopen** a closed vacancy with a new closing date if there were too few suitable applicants.
* **Archive** it when recruitment is finished. An unwanted draft can also be archived (abandoned).

## HR Managers

Vacancies waiting for you are on the **Pending approval** tab, on the home-page tile, and in your email.

Open the vacancy and check the wording, requirements, salary range and closing date, then:

| Button | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Approve and publish** | The vacancy is published immediately and open for applications until the closing date. The recruiter and HOD are emailed. |
| **Return for changes** | Back to Draft for the Recruitment Officer. **Comments required.** |

* You can't approve a vacancy you submitted yourself. Another HR Manager must.
* A vacancy whose closing date has already passed can't be approved. Return it so a new date can be set.

---

## Heads of Department

You can open your department's vacancies to follow their progress, but you can't change them. You're emailed when one is published.

---

## Good to know

* **One vacancy per approved request.** To advertise the same job again later, raise a new recruitment request.
* The **History** box shows every step, including automatic closing (recorded as "System").
* Advertising on the careers page, LinkedIn, Jobberman and so on comes in the next phase (Job Advertisements). Online applications follow in the candidate portal phase.
