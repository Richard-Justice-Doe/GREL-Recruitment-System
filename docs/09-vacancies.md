# 9. Vacancies: user guide

For **Recruitment Officers** (HR Managers and Heads of Department can view vacancies). No technical knowledge needed.

A vacancy is the position as it will be advertised. Every vacancy comes from a recruitment request **approved for recruitment** by the Managing Director (see the [recruitment requests guide](08-recruitment-requests.md)), so nothing can be advertised without the HOD → Recruitment Officer → MD approvals. **The MD's approval is the last one (since 17 November 2026):** the Recruitment Officer publishes the vacancy without any further approval.

```
 Recruitment Officer                                        (automatic or by hand)
 ───────────────────                                        ──────────────────────
 1. Draft ── publish ──► 2. Published ── closing date passes ──► 3. Closed ── archive ──► 4. Archived
                               ▲                                   │
                               └──────── reopen with a new date ───┘
```

**Menu → Vacancies & adverts → Vacancies tab.** Vacancies and job adverts share one menu item; the **Vacancies** and **Job adverts** tabs at the top of the page switch between them.

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Published vacancies, closing soonest first. A red label marks the last 3 days. |
| **Pending approval** | Only vacancies sent to the HR Manager before 17 November 2026 (earlier workflow). The Recruitment Officer can publish them now. |
| **Approved requests awaiting a vacancy** | Requests approved for recruitment that still need a vacancy. Recruitment Officers start here. |
| **All** | Every vacancy, with filters for status, department and search |

## Recruitment Officers

### Convert an approved request into a job vacancy
Recruitment may only begin once the request has the Managing Director's approval (see the [recruitment requests guide](08-recruitment-requests.md)).

1. Open the **Approved requests awaiting a vacancy** tab (or the approved request itself) and click **Convert into vacancy** / **Create the vacancy**. It is also on your Action Center as a **High** priority item.
2. The form is **filled in from the request**: job title, unit, site, reporting manager (the HOD), and from the QF-81-11 form the description of the post (as plain text), educational level, field of study, level of training, years and type of experience, the required certificates, and the driving licence: *Mandatory* adds an essential certification and a knockout question ("Do you hold a valid class C driving licence?"), *Desirable* only a desirable certification, *Not needed* nothing. The department, number of positions and employment type are **fixed**, because that is what was approved.
3. Under **Job requisition**, at the top:
   * the **Job Requisition Number** (e.g. **JRN-2026-0001**) is given automatically when you save;
   * choose the **Vacancy owner**: the Recruitment Officer who runs this recruitment (you by default; it can be changed while the vacancy is a draft);
   * set the **Recruitment closing date**: the last day applicants can apply (two weeks ahead by default);
   * select **Internal or external recruitment**: internal (GREL staff only), external (public), or both.
4. Check and complete the rest:
   * **Job description**: what the applicant will read. Tidy the text copied from the request if needed;
   * **Draft the job description with AI** (if switched on in *AI settings & usage*): add the grade, level and main responsibilities if you like, click **Draft with AI**, check the preview, then **Use this draft**. It fills the job description and the requirements (education, experience, skills, certifications) for you to edit; nothing is saved until you save the form.
   * **Requirements**: the **skills** and **certifications**, **one per line**, separating *essential* (must have) from *desirable* (nice to have). These lists are used later to rank applicants automatically, so keep each item short and specific ("NEBOSH IGC", not "a safety certificate of some kind");
5. Click **Save draft**. The vacancy gets its number, e.g. **VAC-2026-0001**, and its Job Requisition Number.

### Publish the vacancy
Open the draft and click **Publish the vacancy**. **No approval is needed**: the Managing Director's approval of the request was the last one. The vacancy is published straight away and open for applications until the closing date. The HOD is emailed. The history records **Published by the Recruitment Officer**. Your next step is the job advert (see the [job adverts guide](13-job-adverts.md)).

A vacancy still **Pending approval** from before 17 November 2026 can be published the same way. You also get a reminder if one waits.

### After it is published
* **Close now** stops new applications early (e.g. enough good applicants).
* When the closing date passes, the vacancy **closes by itself** within the hour. Applications are accepted for the whole of the closing day. You'll get an email when it closes.
* **Reopen** a closed vacancy with a new closing date if there were too few suitable applicants.
* **Archive** it when recruitment is finished. An unwanted draft can also be archived (abandoned).

---

## HR Managers

Since 17 November 2026 the HR Manager **does not approve vacancies**. Only vacancies sent for HR approval before that date can still be approved (**Approve and publish**) or returned (**Return for changes**, comments required) from the vacancy page; the Recruitment Officer can also simply publish them.

---

## Heads of Department

You can open your department's vacancies to follow their progress, but you can't change them. You're emailed when one is published.

---

## Good to know

* **One vacancy per approved request.** To advertise the same job again later, raise a new recruitment request.
* The **History** box shows every step, including automatic closing (recorded as "System").
* Advertising on the careers page, LinkedIn, Jobberman and so on comes in the next phase (Job Advertisements). Online applications follow in the candidate portal phase.
