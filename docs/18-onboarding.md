# 18. Appointment letters and onboarding: user guide

> **Updated 6 November 2026 (Phase 18):** onboarding now runs through eight stages, with the new employee uploading the **new-hire documents** and HR verifying them, an HR Manager approval, the departmental induction and **activation** of the staff account. The new-hire documents replace the "bring on your first day" checklist items, and *Complete onboarding* is replaced by *Induction complete* and *Activate the employee*. See [24 Employee onboarding](24-onboarding-module.md). The rest of this page (letter, first-day details, checklist) still applies.

Phase 9 takes a candidate who has **accepted the offer** to their first day and beyond:

1. HR **starts the onboarding**, which creates a checklist for HR, the department, IT and the new employee;
2. the HR Manager **issues the appointment letter**, which the new employee **signs online**;
3. everyone ticks off their **checklist items**;
4. HR **completes** the onboarding.

Open everything from **Menu → Onboarding**, from the hiring case (*Start onboarding* / *Open the onboarding*), or from *My tasks* on the Home page.

## 1. Starting the onboarding (Recruitment Officer or HR Manager)

When a candidate accepts an offer:
* the recruiter sees **Onboard *name*** in *My tasks*;
* the candidate appears under **Onboarding → Waiting to start**;
* the hiring case shows **Start onboarding**.

The form is pre-filled. Check:

| Field | Default |
|---|---|
| Start date | the start date in the accepted offer |
| Report at | 08:00 |
| Where to report | the HR office at the vacancy's site |
| Met by | the vacancy's reporting manager |
| Notes | e.g. dress code, transport from the gate |

**Start onboarding** creates the onboarding (`ONB-2026-0001`) and the standard checklist. Each owner gets one email listing their tasks.

## 2. The checklist

| Team | Items | Owner by default | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human Resources | personal file and certificates; induction programme; payroll set-up (bank, SSNIT, GRA, staff number); benefits; GRSMS account changed to a staff account | the vacancy's recruiter | 5 days before to 14 days after the start |
| Department | workspace, tools and PPE; department induction; probation objectives | the department's Head | the day before to 14 days after |
| IT | email account and system access; computer / phone | the Head of the IT department | 2 days and 1 day before |
| New employee (onboardings started before 6 November 2026 only) | Ghana Card, SSNIT number, bank details, original certificates, two passport photographs | nobody: HR ticks each one when received | the first day |

Each item can be marked **Done** (with an optional note, e.g. the new email address) or **Not needed** (a reason is required). A finished item can be reopened. Overdue items are outlined in red.

**Who can tick an item:** its owner, the department's Head (department items), or HR (any item).

**HR can also:**
* **reassign** an item to any active staff member, or change its due date. The new owner is emailed and can then open the onboarding;
* **add** an item (e.g. *Safety briefing at the APM factory*) or **remove** one that is still to do;
* change the **first-day details** and record the **staff number** from payroll.

Moving the start date moves every open item's due date by the same number of days.

Items assigned to you appear in *My tasks* on the Home page, with a count next to **Onboarding** in the menu. Staff who are not in HR see the Onboarding menu while they have items to do.

## 3. The appointment letter (HR Manager)

On the onboarding page, an HR Manager writes the **conditions of appointment** (pre-filled with the offer's terms, or with a standard text covering probation, hours, benefits and notice) and clicks **Issue the appointment letter**. The letter gets its own number (`APT-2026-0001`), and the new employee is emailed.

The letter shows the position, department, place of work, reporting manager, monthly salary, date of appointment, the conditions, and where and when to report. **Letter preview** shows it exactly as the new employee sees it; it can be printed.

Until the new employee signs, the HR Manager can **issue a revised letter**. It keeps the same number, and the new employee is emailed again.

## 4. The new employee's side

On *My applications*, the new employee sees **Your first day**:
* the date and time, the place, who meets them and any notes;
* **Please bring**: the documents on the checklist, each marked *(received)* once HR has ticked it.

**Read and sign my appointment letter** opens the letter. To sign, they:
1. type their full name exactly as it appears on the letter (capitals and extra spaces don't matter);
2. tick *I accept the appointment on these terms*;
3. click **Sign the letter**.

GRSMS records the time, the name typed and the network address. HR (the person who started the onboarding and the letter's issuer) is emailed.

**A signed letter is final:** it can no longer be revised, and the start date it states can no longer be changed. The time, place and other first-day details can still change. If they change after the letter is issued, the new employee is emailed.

## 5. Completing or cancelling (Recruitment Officer or HR Manager)

**Since Phase 18** there is no single *Complete onboarding* button: HR closes the induction once every item is done or not needed and the start date has come, then **activates** the employee with their staff number. See [24 Employee onboarding](24-onboarding-module.md#induction-and-activation).


**Cancel onboarding** (e.g. the new employee did not report) needs a reason. The department's Head is emailed, and the new employee no longer sees the appointment.

Each step (started, letter issued, letter signed, completed, cancelled) is written on the application's history and in the audit trail. The application stays **Hired**.

## Who can do what

| | Recruitment Officer | HR Manager | Department HOD | Other staff | New employee |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| See onboardings | ✅ | ✅ | own department | if given an item | |
| Start, change first-day details and the checklist, complete, cancel | ✅ | ✅ | | | |
| Issue / revise the appointment letter | | ✅ | | | |
| Tick off checklist items | ✅ any | ✅ any | department items and their own | their own | |
| Read and sign the letter; see the first-day information | | | | | ✅ own |

The MD and HR Administrator can see every onboarding (read only). Nobody manages the onboarding of their own application.

## Good to know

* **The candidate's account is not changed automatically.** After the first day, an HR Administrator gives the account the *Employee* role (and removes *Candidate*) in **Users & roles**. This is an HR item on the checklist.
* **The Head of IT** receives the IT items because GRSMS has no separate IT role. Make sure the *Information Technology* department (code `IT`) has a Head set in **Departments**, or reassign the IT items by hand.
* Every onboarding records the **division, department and requesting HOD** of its vacancy, so it can be reported on by division and department (Phase 11).
