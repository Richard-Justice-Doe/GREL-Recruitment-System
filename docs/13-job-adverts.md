# 13. Job adverts: user guide

Once a vacancy is **published** (the HR Manager has approved it), the Recruitment Officer advertises it. GRSMS publishes adverts on its own **internal portal** and **careers page**, and prepares ready-to-paste text for **LinkedIn, Indeed, Jobberman and newspapers**.

**Who can do what**

| | Recruitment Officer | HR Manager | Head of Department | MD, HR Administrator |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| See adverts | all | all | own departments | all |
| Create, edit, publish, close, clone, record postings | ✅ | ✅ | | |

## The channels

| Channel | How it is published | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal portal** | Automatically, while the advert is live | Signed-in GREL staff, on the careers page (marked *GREL staff only*) |
| **Company website** | Automatically, while the advert is live | **Anyone**, on the public careers page: `https://<grsms address>/Careers`. Link GREL's website to this address. |
| **LinkedIn, Indeed, Jobberman** | By you: copy the ready-made text into the site, then record the link | Visitors to those sites |
| **Newspaper** | By you: send the ready-made text to the paper, then record the edition | Readers |

GRSMS is not connected to LinkedIn, Indeed, Jobberman or the newspapers, and stores no passwords for them.

A vacancy marked **Internal (GREL staff only)** can only be advertised on the internal portal.

## Life of an advert

```
Draft ──Publish (start date today)───────► Live ──end date passes / vacancy closes / Close──► Closed
  │                                          ▲
  └──Publish (start date later)──► Scheduled ┘ (goes live by itself on the start date)
                                      └──Cancel schedule──► Draft
Any advert ──Clone──► a new Draft
```

## Step by step

### 1. Create the advert
1. Open the vacancy (**Vacancies → Open**) and click **Create advert** in the *Job adverts* box. The button only appears while the vacancy is published and open.
2. The form is filled in from the vacancy: title, advert text (description, requirements, salary if *show on adverts* was ticked, location) and *How to apply*. Edit anything you like.
3. Choose the **start date** (today = live as soon as you publish; a later date = scheduled) and **end date** (at the latest the vacancy's closing date).
4. Tick the **channels**. The internal portal and company website are ticked to start with.
5. **Save draft**. The advert gets its number, e.g. **ADV-2026-0001**.

### 2. Publish
On the advert page, click **Publish now** (start date today) or **Schedule** (later start date).
* **Live:** it appears on the careers page (website channel) and/or the internal jobs list straight away. You, the vacancy's recruiter and the Head of Department are emailed. The email lists the external channels still to post.
* **Scheduled:** it goes live by itself on the start date. Until then you can still edit it, or **Cancel schedule** to make it a draft again.

You cannot publish if the vacancy is no longer published (e.g. it was closed) or its closing date has passed. The page tells you why.

### 3. Post on the external channels
For each external channel the advert page shows **Copy-ready text**:
1. Click **Copy** and paste it into LinkedIn, Indeed or Jobberman, or send it to the newspaper. The text ends with the link to the careers page and the closing date.
2. Once it is up, paste the posting's link (or the newspaper name and date of the edition) and click **Record as posted**.

The *Job adverts* list shows how many channels are still **to post**, and a reminder sits above the list.

### 4. Closing
* An advert closes **by itself** the day after its end date, or as soon as its vacancy closes (the check runs every hour). It then disappears from the careers page.
* You can **Close now** at any time.
* External postings are **not** removed automatically. Take the advert down from each site, then click **Record as taken down**.

### 5. Clone
**Clone into a new draft** copies the text and channels into a new advert for the same vacancy, with new dates (today until the vacancy's closing date). Use it to re-advertise, for example after the vacancy is reopened, or to fix a mistake in a closed advert.

## The careers page

* **Public** (no sign-in): `/Careers` lists every live advert on the company-website channel, with department, division, location, employment type and closing date. Each job has its own page, `/Careers/Details/ADV-2026-0001`, which is the link used in the copy-ready texts.
* **Staff:** signed-in staff also see internal-portal adverts, marked *GREL staff only*. The sign-in page and the candidate home page link to the careers page.
* **Applying online** opens in Phase 5 (candidate portal). Until then the page shows the advert's *How to apply* text and invites visitors to create a candidate account.

## Reports

The *Job adverts* list filters by **division, department, channel, period** (adverts running in that period) and **status**, and searches advert numbers, vacancy numbers and titles. Each advert keeps the division, department and requesting HOD of its vacancy.
