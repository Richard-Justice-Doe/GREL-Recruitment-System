# 16. Interviews, assessments and scorecards: user guide

Phase 7 covers what happens after shortlisting: **written tests, practical assessments and interviews**, each with a **panel** that scores the applicant on a **scorecard**. GREL requires the department's **Head of Department** on every panel, and the HOD reviews the scorecards.

## 1. Schedule a session (Recruitment Officer or HR Manager)

Open a **shortlisted** application (from *Applications* or the shortlisting board) and click **Schedule an interview or assessment**.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Interview, written test or practical assessment |
| **How** | In person, video call or phone call |
| **Date and time** | GMT (Ghana time). A session that already took place (up to yesterday) can be recorded too. |
| **Duration** and **where** | Room and building, or the video link |
| **Instructions** | Shown to the applicant, e.g. *bring your original certificates* |
| **Panel** | 2 to 8 GREL staff. The department's **HOD is ticked for you and must stay**. Choose one **chair**. The applicant can never sit on their own panel. |
| **Scorecard criteria** | One per line, `name \| weight`; the weights must add up to **100**. Five sensible defaults are filled in. |

Click **Schedule and send invitations**. Then:
* the applicant is emailed the date, place and instructions, and sees them on their tracker;
* each panel member is emailed;
* the application moves to **Interview**.

An applicant can have several **rounds**, for example a written test followed by an interview.

## 2. Score (panel members)

Any GREL staff member can sit on a panel. Open **Menu → Interviews**:
* **My panels** lists your sessions.
* **Scorecards to do** lists sessions that have started and still need your scorecard. The red number in the menu and the *My tasks* list on Home show the same.

On the session page, the scorecard **opens when the session starts**:
1. Rate each criterion **1 to 5** (1 poor, 2 below expectations, 3 meets, 4 above, 5 outstanding).
2. Note **strengths** and **concerns**. Concerns are required if you do not recommend the applicant.
3. Choose an overall **recommendation**: strongly recommend, recommend, recommend with reservations, or do not recommend.
4. Click **Submit scorecard**.

GRSMS works out the **weighted score out of 100**: each rating ÷ 5 × the criterion's weight, added up. For example, 4/5 on a 60-weight criterion plus 5/5 on a 40-weight one gives 48 + 40 = **88**.

**Independent scoring:** panel members see the other scorecards **only after submitting their own**, so nobody is swayed. You can update your scorecard until the session is marked completed.

## 3. Close the session (Recruitment Officer or HR Manager)

| Action | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Mark completed** | After it started, with at least one scorecard | Scorecards become final |
| **Reschedule** | Before any scorecard is in | New time and place; the applicant and panel are emailed |
| **Applicant did not attend** | After it started | Recorded on the application's history |
| **Cancel** | Any time while scheduled, with a reason | The applicant and panel are emailed |

## 4. Review the results (HOD, recruiters, HR Manager, MD)

* **Session page:** every scorecard side by side, with ratings per criterion, weighted scores, recommendations and comments, plus the panel average. The department's HOD, Recruitment Officers, HR Managers, the MD and HR Administrators always see all scorecards.
* **Interview results** (from the session page, the application page or the shortlisting board): every applicant for the vacancy who had a session, ranked by their **average interview score**, with their screening score, each round and how the panel recommended. This is the starting point for the hiring recommendation (Phase 8).

## What the applicant sees

The date, time, place, how long it takes and your instructions, on their tracker and in their email. **Never** scores, ratings, recommendations or comments.
